# Publication and history

The source is distributed under Apache-2.0; see LICENSE. Dependencies retain their own licenses and required notices. Deployment operators must configure their own credentials, domains, contact details, privacy policy, and service terms. No hosted service is offered by this source distribution.

Repository history was privacy-normalized: authors and committers use the maintainer GitHub noreply identity and recorded AI coauthors retain credit, commit messages were shortened, and calendar dates are retained while author and committer clock times are randomized between 18:00 and 23:59:59 in America/Los_Angeles for privacy. These are not the original activity times. Contribution roles and commit ancestry are retained.

Never import recovery branches, old tags, stashes, environment files, local agent settings, or private operational reports into a public repository. Scan every reachable commit and image before publication.
