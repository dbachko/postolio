# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 28, 2025  
**Last Updated:** July 28, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to Postolio ("we," "our," or "us"). Postolio is an AI-powered Instagram content generation and management platform that helps users create, schedule, and publish Instagram content automatically. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

By using Postolio, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, password (encrypted), profile information
- **Contact Details**: Email address, phone number (if provided)
- **Payment Information**: Billing address, payment method details (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
- **Communication Data**: Messages you send to us, support tickets, feedback

### 1.2 Instagram Data
- **Profile Information**: Instagram username, profile picture, bio, follower count, following count
- **Content Data**: Your Instagram posts, captions, hashtags, images, videos, and their metadata
- **Analytics Data**: Post performance metrics, engagement rates, reach, impressions, audience demographics
- **Account Connection Data**: OAuth tokens and refresh tokens for Instagram Graph API access

### 1.3 Generated Content
- **AI-Generated Content**: Captions, hashtags, and images created by our AI system for your brands
- **Brand Analysis Data**: Style preferences, content themes, and brand voice profiles derived from your Instagram content
- **Campaign Information**: Automated posting schedules, campaign configurations, and performance data

### 1.4 Usage and Technical Data
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
- **Usage Analytics**: Pages visited, features used, time spent on platform, click-through rates
- **Log Data**: Server logs, error reports, system performance data
- **Cookies and Tracking**: Session cookies, preference cookies, analytics cookies

### 1.5 Third-Party Service Data
- **OpenAI Integration**: Content generation requests and responses (anonymized where possible)
- **Meta/Facebook Integration**: Data accessed through Instagram Graph API for content publishing
- **Analytics Services**: Performance metrics from integrated analytics tools

- **Content Generation**: Creating personalized AI-generated posts, captions, and hashtags

### 2.3 Communication
- **Service Communications**: Sending important updates about your account or our services
- **Marketing Communications**: Promotional emails and product updates (with your consent)
- **Security Notifications**: Alerting you to security issues or suspicious account activity

### 2.4 Legal and Compliance
- **Legal Obligations**: Complying with applicable laws, regulations, and legal requests
- **Security**: Protecting against fraud, abuse, and security threats
- **Terms Enforcement**: Ensuring compliance with our Terms of Service

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Union, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

- **Contract Performance**: Processing necessary to provide our services (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
- **Legitimate Interest**: Analytics, security, service improvement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
- **Consent**: Marketing communications, optional features (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
- **Legal Obligation**: Compliance with applicable laws (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication for our team
- **Infrastructure Security**: Secure cloud hosting with regular security audits
- **Database Security**: Encrypted databases with restricted access
- **API Security**: OAuth 2.0 authentication, rate limiting, and secure API endpoints

### 5.2 Instagram API Security
- **Token Management**: Secure storage and regular rotation of OAuth tokens
- **Scope Limitation**: We only request necessary Instagram permissions
- **Data Minimization**: We collect only the Instagram data required for our services

### 5.3 AI Content Security
- **Content Privacy**: AI-generated content is processed securely and not shared with unauthorized parties
- **Model Security**: Our AI models are updated regularly to maintain security and accuracy

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods
- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active
- **Instagram Data**: Retained for up to 2 years after account closure for legitimate business purposes
- **Generated Content**: Retained for 1 year after creation unless deleted earlier by you
- **Analytics Data**: Aggregated analytics retained for up to 3 years
- **Support Data**: Customer support communications retained for 2 years

### 6.2 Deletion Requests
You can request deletion of your data at any time. We will delete your personal data within 30 days of your request, except where retention is required by law.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 General Rights
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update or correct inaccurate personal data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Data Portability**: Request your data in a portable format
- **Withdrawal of Consent**: Withdraw consent for processing based on consent

### 7.2 GDPR Rights (EU Users)
- **Right to Object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Right to Restriction**: Request restriction of processing under certain circumstances
- **Data Protection Officer**: Contact our DPO for privacy-related concerns

### 7.3 CCPA Rights (California Users)
- **Right to Know**: Categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
- **Right to Delete**: Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out**: Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: Equal service regardless of exercising privacy rights

### 7.4 Exercising Your Rights
To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@postolio.example or through your account settings. We will respond to your request within 30 days (or as required by applicable law).
