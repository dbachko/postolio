# Data Deletion Instructions

**Last Updated:** January 10, 2025  
**Effective Date:** January 10, 2025

## How to Delete Your Data

Postolio respects your right to privacy and provides multiple ways to delete your personal data and account. This page explains how to request deletion of your information from our service.

## For Registered Users

### Delete Your Account from Settings

1. **Log in** to your Postolio account at https://postolio.example
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Privacy & Security**
3. Click **Delete My Account**
4. **Review** the information about what will be deleted
5. **Complete the exit survey** (optional) to help us improve
6. **Enter your password** to confirm
7. Click **Permanently Delete Account**

### What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we remove:
- Your user profile and account credentials
- All brands you've created
- All generated content, captions, and images
- Campaign data and templates
- Instagram connection and cached data
- Analytics and usage history
- Payment information and subscription details

### Recovery Period

**Important:** After initiating deletion, you have a **30-day grace period** to recover your account:
- Your account will be deactivated immediately
- You can reactivate within 30 days by logging in
- After 30 days, deletion is permanent and irreversible

## For Non-Registered Users

If you don't have an account but believe we may have your information (for example, through Instagram integration), you can request deletion:

### Email Request

Send an email to **privacy@postolio.example** with:
- Subject: "Data Deletion Request"
- Your full name
- Email address associated with any data
- Instagram username (if applicable)
- Description of your relationship with Postolio

We will respond within 48 hours to verify your identity and process your request.

## For Instagram/Facebook Users

If you connected your Instagram account to Postolio through Facebook Login:

### Method 1: Through Instagram Settings

1. Go to your **Instagram Settings**
2. Navigate to **Apps and Websites**
3. Find **Postolio** in the list
4. Click **Remove**
5. Select **Delete** to remove all data

### Method 2: Direct Request

Visit our data deletion page at:
**https://postolio.example/legal/data-deletion**

Enter your Instagram username or the email associated with your Facebook account, and we'll process your deletion request.

## Meta Platform Deletion Callback

When you remove Postolio from your Instagram/Facebook account, we automatically receive a deletion request and will:
1. Delete your Instagram access tokens
2. Remove cached Instagram data
3. Delete any content scheduled for Instagram
4. Send you a confirmation email

## Processing Timeline

- **Immediate**: Account deactivation (for registered users)
- **24 hours**: Primary data deletion begins
- **30 days**: Complete removal from all systems
- **90 days**: Anonymized analytics data removed

## Data Retention Exceptions

We may retain certain information for:
- **Legal compliance**: As required by law
- **Fraud prevention**: To prevent abuse of our service
- **Anonymized analytics**: Aggregated data with no personal identifiers

## Verification Process

To protect your privacy, we verify all deletion requests:

### For Registered Users
- Password confirmation required
- Email notification sent to account email

### For Non-Registered Users
- Identity verification via email
- May request additional information to confirm identity

## Your Rights

Under privacy laws including GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:
- Request deletion of your personal data
- Receive confirmation of deletion
- Lodge a complaint with supervisory authorities

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I delete specific content without deleting my account?
Yes, you can delete individual campaigns, content, or brands from within your account settings without deleting your entire account.

### Can I export my data before deletion?
Yes, we recommend downloading your data before deletion. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Export My Data.

### What happens to content already posted to Instagram?
Content already published to Instagram remains on Instagram. You must delete it directly from Instagram if desired.

### Can I recover my account after deletion?
You have 30 days to recover your account by logging in. After 30 days, recovery is not possible.

### How do I know my data has been deleted?
You will receive email confirmations at each stage:
1. When deletion is requested
2. When the 30-day period expires
3. When deletion is complete
